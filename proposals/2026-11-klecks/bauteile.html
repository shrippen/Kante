Kante for Klecks: four QML gaps

Klecks (a desktop app that sorts video frames) built these four locally. They are QML only; the web has .drop and .steps already. Live: qml/demo/Gallery.qml (sections navigation, data and lists, surfaces); tests: qml/tests/tst_kante_klecks.qml.

ComponentAPINote
KanteLineChartthresholds [{value, color, series, label}], xStart, xEnd, xTitle, yTitleDashed reference lines; axis captions with room kept by the chart (padTop, padBottom)
KanteFileDrophint, chooseText, dropped(urls), chooseRequested(), dragOverFiles from outside the app; KanteDropZone stays the tile placeholder
KanteTilesource, placeholder, captionedImage or neutral placeholder; name and figure optional
KanteStepsclickable, activated(index), errorsSteps as buttons; a failed step in the negative colour with an exclamation mark
