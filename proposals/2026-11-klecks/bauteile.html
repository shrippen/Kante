Kante for Klecks: QML gaps

Klecks (a desktop app that sorts video frames) built these locally. They are QML only; the web has .drop and .steps already. Live: qml/demo/Gallery.qml (sections navigation, data and lists, surfaces); tests: qml/tests/tst_kante_klecks.qml, qml/tests/tst_kante_scatter.qml.
